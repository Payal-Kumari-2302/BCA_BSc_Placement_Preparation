# 📚 Idioms and Phrases – TCS Placement Preparation

A priority-wise collection of 300 important Idioms and Phrases for TCS NQT, TCS BCA Ignite, and other IT placement examinations.

## 🎯 Objective

- Learn important idioms and their meanings.
- Improve English vocabulary and verbal ability.
- Prepare for TCS NQT and other placement exams.
- Practise idioms through MCQs and previous-year questions.

## 📌 Preparation Strategy

**Daily Target: 10 Idioms**

| Target | Total Idioms | Preparation Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| Minimum Target | 50 | 5 Days |
| Recommended Target | 100 | 10 Days |
| Maximum Recommended Target | 150 | 15 Days |
| Extra Practice | 151–300 | 15 Additional Days |

### ⭐ How Many Idioms Should You Complete?

- **First 50:** Must complete.
- **First 100:** Strongly recommended.
- **First 150:** Maximum recommended target for focused placement preparation.
- **151–300:** Optional extra practice.

**Important:** This is a suggested study priority, not an official TCS frequency ranking. Completing 150 idioms with proper revision and MCQ practice is more useful than memorising 300 without understanding them.

# 3. How to Study 10 Idioms Daily

Follow this routine every day:

1. Learn 10 new idioms with their meanings.
2. Read every idiom aloud.
3. Write each idiom and meaning once.
4. Make sentences using at least 5 idioms.
5. Revise the previous day's 10 idioms.
6. Solve 10–15 MCQs related to idioms.

**Daily time:** Approximately 10  minutes.

## 4. Revision Strategy

| Revision | What to Do |
|---|---|
| Daily | Revise yesterday's 10 idioms |
| After 5 Days | Revise all 50 idioms |
| After 10 Days | Revise all 100 idioms |
| After 15 Days | Revise the first 150 idioms |
| After 30 Days | Revise all 300 if completed |

## ⭐ Final Recommendation

For TCS NQT, TCS BCA Ignite and other IT placements:

- Complete the first **100 idioms** as your essential target.
- Try to complete the first **150 idioms** as your main target.
- Study the remaining 150 only if you have enough preparation time.
- Practise MCQs regularly from IndiaBix, GeeksforGeeks and PrepInsta.
- Focus on meaning, usage and revision rather than memorising the list blindly.

**Remember:** 150 well-prepared idioms + regular MCQ practice is a better target than 300 unrevised idioms.
