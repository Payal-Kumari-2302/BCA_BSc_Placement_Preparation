# 📚 One Word Substitution – TCS Placement Preparation

A priority-wise collection of 300 important One Word Substitutions for TCS NQT, TCS BCA Ignite and other IT placement examinations.

## 🎯 Objective

- Learn important One Word Substitutions.
- Improve English vocabulary and verbal ability.
- Prepare for placement-level English questions.
- Practise MCQs from GeeksforGeeks, IndiaBix and PrepInsta.

## 📌 Preparation Strategy

**Daily Target: 10 Words**

| Target | Total Words | Preparation Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| Minimum Target | 50 | 5 Days |
| Recommended Target | 100 | 10 Days |
| Maximum Recommended Target | 150 | 15 Days |
| Extra Practice | 151–300 | 15 Additional Days |

### ⭐ How Many Words Should You Complete?

- **First 50:** Must complete.
- **First 100:** Strongly recommended.
- **First 150:** Maximum recommended target for focused placement preparation.
- **151–300:** Optional extra practice.

**Note:** This is a suggested study priority, not an official TCS frequency ranking.

# 3. How to Study 10 Words Daily

Follow this routine:

1. Learn 10 new One Word Substitutions.
2. Understand the complete phrase and its one-word answer.
3. Read each word aloud.
4. Write each word and its definition once.
5. Revise the previous day's 10 words.
6. Solve 10–15 MCQs.

**Daily time:** Approximately 10 minutes.

# ⭐ Final Recommendation

For TCS NQT, TCS BCA Ignite and other IT placements:

- Complete the first **100 words** as your essential target.
- Try to complete the first **150 words** as your main target.
- Practise the remaining 150 only if you have enough time.
- Solve topic-wise MCQs from IndiaBix, GeeksforGeeks and PrepInsta.
- Focus on understanding the definition and identifying the correct word.

**Remember:** 150 well-prepared One Word Substitutions with regular MCQ practice are better than memorising 300 words without revision.
