# SECTION 6: HOW TO PREPARE EFFECTIVELY

1. Learn the Hindi meaning of every word.
2. Learn at least two synonyms and one antonym.
3. Read the word aloud to improve pronunciation.
4. Use each difficult word in a sentence.
5. Revise words after 1 day, 3 days and 7 days.
6. Practice MCQs without looking at the answers.
7. Maintain a separate list of incorrect answers.
8. Revise incorrect words every Sunday.

# SECTION 8: IMPORTANT PREPARATION RULES

- Do not memorize words without understanding their meanings.
- Do not depend only on difficult vocabulary.
- Give priority to common and moderate-level words.
- Practice contextual synonyms and antonyms.
- Avoid repeating words you already know.
- Maintain an error notebook.
- Take one vocabulary test every day.
