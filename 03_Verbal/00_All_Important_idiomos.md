# 4. Practice Instructions

1. Learn 10 idioms daily.
2. Understand the meaning of each idiom.
3. Revise previously studied idioms regularly.
4. Practise MCQs after completing each section.
5. Attempt questions without looking at the answers.
6. Maintain a separate list of incorrect answers.
7. Practise previous-year placement questions.
8. Take mixed verbal ability mock tests.
