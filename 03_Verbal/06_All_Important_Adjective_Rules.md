# Adjective – Important Grammar Rules

**Target Exams:** TCS Ignite, TCS NQT, Wipro, Infosys, Accenture, Cognizant & Deloitte.

## 1. Adjective Rules

### Rule 1: Adjective + Noun
An adjective usually comes before a noun.

**Structure:** Adjective + Noun

- She is an intelligent girl.
- He bought a beautiful car.

### Rule 2: Linking Verb + Adjective
An adjective comes after linking verbs such as is, am, are, was, were, seem, look, feel, become.

- She is intelligent.
- He looks happy.
- The food smells delicious.

### Rule 3: Adjective After Certain Verbs
Some verbs are followed by adjectives.

**Verbs:** Make, Keep, Find, Consider, Prove.

- They made him angry.
- Keep your room clean.
- I found the question difficult.

### Rule 4: Adjective + Preposition
Certain adjectives are followed by specific prepositions.

| Adjective | Preposition |
|---|---|
| Good | At |
| Interested | In |
| Afraid | Of |
| Famous | For |
| Proud | Of |
| Different | From |
| Married | To |
| Responsible | For |
| Similar | To |
| Capable | Of |

### Rule 5: Adjective of Quantity
Use adjectives of quantity with uncountable nouns.

- Much water
- Little time
- Enough money

### Rule 6: Adjective of Number
Use adjectives of number with countable nouns.

- Many students
- Few books
- Several questions

### Rule 7: Comparative Degree
Use comparative adjectives to compare two people or things.

**Structure:** Subject + Verb + Comparative Adjective + Than

- Rahul is taller than Aman.
- This book is better than that one.

### Rule 8: Superlative Degree
Use superlative adjectives to compare more than two people or things.

**Structure:** Subject + Verb + The + Superlative Adjective

- Rahul is the tallest boy in the class.
- She is the most intelligent student.

### Rule 9: As + Adjective + As
Use the positive degree to show equality.

**Structure:** As + Adjective + As

- She is as intelligent as her sister.

### Rule 10: Too + Adjective + To
Use this structure to express something excessive or impossible.

**Structure:** Too + Adjective + To + V1

- He is too weak to walk.

### Rule 11: So + Adjective + That
Use this structure to show a result.

**Structure:** So + Adjective + That + Clause

- He was so tired that he slept immediately.

### Rule 12: Such + A/An + Adjective + Noun
Use such to emphasize a noun.

**Structure:** Such + A/An + Adjective + Singular Countable Noun

- She is such a beautiful girl.

### Rule 13: Adjective Order
When multiple adjectives describe one noun, generally follow this order:

**Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Colour → Origin → Material → Purpose → Noun**

Example:
- She bought a beautiful small new red Italian leather bag.

### Rule 14: Enough + Noun / Adjective + Enough

- We have enough money.
- He is strong enough to lift it.

### Rule 15: Less / Fewer

- Less → Uncountable nouns.
- Fewer → Countable nouns.

Examples:
- We have less water.
- There are fewer students.

## 2. Common Adjective Errors

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| She is more taller than me. | She is taller than me. |
| He is the most tallest boy. | He is the tallest boy. |
| She is senior than me. | She is senior to me. |
| He is good in English. | He is good at English. |
| I am interested on music. | I am interested in music. |
| He is enough strong. | He is strong enough. |
| She is a such beautiful girl. | She is such a beautiful girl. |
| He is too weak that he cannot walk. | He is so weak that he cannot walk. |
