# Pronoun – Complete Grammar Rules & Error Spotting

**Target Exams:** TCS Ignite, TCS NQT, Wipro, Infosys, Accenture, Cognizant, Deloitte & Genpact.

## 🔴 PART 1 – Important Pronoun Rules

### Rule 1: Subjective Pronouns
Use subject pronouns as the subject of a sentence.

**I, We, You, He, She, It, They**

- He is my brother. ✅
- Him is my brother. ❌

### Rule 2: Objective Pronouns
Use object pronouns after verbs and prepositions.

**Me, Us, You, Him, Her, It, Them**

- She called me. ✅
- She called I. ❌

### Rule 3: Possessive Pronouns
Possessive pronouns show ownership and are not followed by nouns.

| Possessive Adjective | Possessive Pronoun |
|---|---|
| My | Mine |
| Our | Ours |
| Your | Yours |
| His | His |
| Her | Hers |
| Their | Theirs |

- This is my book. ✅
- This book is mine. ✅
- This is mine book. ❌

### Rule 4: Reflexive Pronouns
Use reflexive pronouns when the subject and object refer to the same person.

**Myself, Yourself, Himself, Herself, Itself, Ourselves, Yourselves, Themselves**

- She hurt herself. ✅
- She hurt himself. ❌

**Important:** Myself cannot replace I or me.

- Myself Payal. ❌
- I am Payal. ✅

### Rule 5: Each / Every / Everyone
These generally take singular verbs.

- Each student has a book. ✅
- Everyone is present. ✅
- Every student have a pen. ❌

### Rule 6: Neither / Either
When used independently, neither and either generally take singular verbs.

- Neither of them is ready. ✅
- Either of the answers is correct. ✅

### Rule 7: Pronoun-Antecedent Agreement
A pronoun must agree with its antecedent in number and person.

- The boys completed their work. ✅
- The boys completed his work. ❌

### Rule 8: Who / Whom
- Who = Subject.
- Whom = Object.

Examples:
- Who called you?
- Whom did you meet?

### Rule 9: Whose / Who's
- Whose = Possession.
- Who's = Who is / Who has.

Examples:
- Whose bag is this?
- Who's coming today?

### Rule 10: Relative Pronouns

| Pronoun | Usage |
|---|---|
| Who | Person (subject) |
| Whom | Person (object) |
| Which | Animals and things |
| That | Persons, animals and things |
| Whose | Possession |

Example: The girl who won the prize is my sister.

### Rule 11: Either...or / Neither...nor
When subjects differ in number, the verb generally agrees with the nearer subject.

- Neither Ram nor his friends are present. ✅
- Neither his friends nor Ram is present. ✅

### Rule 12: One / One's
Maintain consistency when one is used as a pronoun.

- One should respect one's parents. ✅

### Rule 13: Between You and Me
After a preposition, use an objective pronoun.

- Between you and me. ✅
- Between you and I. ❌

### Rule 14: Let
Use an objective pronoun after let.

- Let him speak. ✅
- Let he speak. ❌

### Rule 15: This / That / These / Those

| Pronoun | Usage |
|---|---|
| This | Singular, near |
| That | Singular, far |
| These | Plural, near |
| Those | Plural, far |

### Rule 16: Another / Other / Others

| Word | Usage |
|---|---|
| Another | One more |
| Other | Additional or different |
| Others | Other people or things |

### Rule 17: Reciprocal Pronouns
- Each other = Traditionally used for two.
- One another = Traditionally used for more than two.

### Rule 18: Emphatic Pronouns
Used to emphasize a person or thing.

- I myself completed the work.
- The principal himself attended the meeting.

### Rule 19: Than
Use the pronoun according to the implied sentence structure.

- She is taller than I am. ✅
- She is taller than me. ✅ (Common usage)

### Rule 20: Distributive Pronouns
Each, either and neither refer to individual members and generally take singular verbs.

- Each of the students is intelligent. ✅
- Either of the options is correct. ✅

# 🔴 PART 2 – Error Spotting Practice

**Instruction:** Identify the part containing the grammatical error.

**Options:**
- A) First Part
- B) Second Part
- C) Third Part

## Priority 1 – Basic Pronoun Rules

**Q1.**  
(A) Me and my brother / (B) went to the market / (C) yesterday / (D) No error.

**Answer: A**  
Correction: My brother and I went to the market yesterday.

**Q2.**  
(A) She gave / (B) the book to he / (C) yesterday / (D) No error.

**Answer: B**  
Correction: She gave the book to him yesterday.

**Q3.**  
(A) This is / (B) mine notebook / (C) on the table / (D) No error.

**Answer: B**  
Correction: This is my notebook on the table.

**Q4.**  
(A) She hurt / (B) himself / (C) while playing / (D) No error.

**Answer: B**  
Correction: She hurt herself while playing.

**Q5.**

|---|---|
| I / Me | Subject / Object |
| My / Mine | Before noun / Without noun |
| He / Him | Subject / Object |
| She / Her | Subject / Object |
| Who / Whom | Subject / Object |
| Whose / Who's | Possession / Who is |
| Each / Every | Singular verb |
| Neither / Either | Generally singular when used alone |
| Myself | Reflexive or emphatic use |
| Their / Theirs | Before noun / Without noun |
| This / These | Singular / Plural |
| That / Those | Singular / Plural |
