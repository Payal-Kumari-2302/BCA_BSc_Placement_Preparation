# Subject–Verb Agreement – Important Grammar Rules

**Target Exams:** TCS Ignite, TCS NQT, Wipro, Infosys, Accenture, Cognizant, Deloitte & Other IT Companies.


## Definition
Subject–Verb Agreement means the verb must agree with its subject in number and person (singular subject → singular verb; plural subject → plural verb).

## 🔴 PART 1 – Most Important Rules

### Rule 1: Singular and Plural Subjects
**Structure:** Singular Subject + Singular Verb | Plural Subject + Plural Verb

- He plays cricket.
- They play cricket.

### Rule 2: Two Subjects Joined by AND
**Structure:** A + AND + B + Plural Verb

- Ram and Shyam are friends.
- My brother and sister work here.

**Exception:** If two nouns refer to one person or one thing, use a singular verb.

- The poet and singer is famous.

### Rule 3: Either...Or / Neither...Nor
**Structure:** Either/Neither + A + Or/Nor + B + Verb (Agrees with the nearest subject)

- Either Ram or his friends are coming.
- Neither the students nor the teacher is present.

### Rule 4: Each / Every
**Structure:** Each/Every + Singular Noun + Singular Verb

- Each student has a book.
- Every employee works hard.

### Rule 5: Everyone / Everybody / Someone / Nobody
**Structure:** Indefinite Pronoun + Singular Verb

- Everyone is ready.
- Nobody knows the answer.
- Somebody has called you.

### Rule 6: One of
**Structure:** One of + Plural Noun + Singular Verb

- One of my friends is a programmer.
- One of the students has won the prize.

### Rule 7: Along With / As Well As / Together With
**Structure:** Subject + Along With/As Well As/Together With + Noun + Verb (Agrees with the first subject)

- Ram, along with his friends, is coming.
- The teacher as well as the students is responsible.

### Rule 8: Collective Nouns
**Structure:** Collective Noun + Singular Verb (when acting as one unit)

- The team is playing well.
- The committee has made a decision.

### Rule 9: Uncountable Nouns
**Structure:** Uncountable Noun + Singular Verb

- Information is important.
- Advice is useful.
- Furniture is expensive.

### Rule 10: There / Here
**Structure:** There/Here + Verb + Actual Subject

- There is a book on the table.
- There are five books on the table.
- Here comes the bus.

### Rule 11: A Number of / The Number of
**Structure:**
- A number of + Plural Noun + Plural Verb
- The number of + Plural Noun + Singular Verb

- A number of students are absent.
- The number of students is increasing.

### Rule 12: More Than One
**Structure:** More Than One + Singular Noun + Singular Verb

- More than one student has applied.
- More than one employee is absent.

### Rule 13: Many a
**Structure:** Many a + Singular Noun + Singular Verb

- Many a student has failed.
- Many a person has tried.

### Rule 14: Time, Money and Distance
**Structure:** Amount/Measurement Considered as One Unit + Singular Verb

- Ten kilometres is a long distance.
- Five hundred rupees is enough.
- Two hours is sufficient.

### Rule 15: Relative Pronouns (Who / Which / That)
**Structure:** Who/Which/That + Verb (Agrees with the noun it refers to)

- She is one of the students who work hard.
- He is the only student who works hard.

## 🟡 PART 2 – Important Exam Exceptions

| Subject | Verb |
|---|---|
| Mathematics | Singular |
| Physics | Singular |
| News | Singular |
| Politics (as a subject) | Singular |
| Police | Plural |
| Cattle | Plural |
| People | Plural |
| Scissors | Plural |
| Trousers | Plural |
| The United States | Singular (as a country) |

**Examples:**
- Mathematics is an interesting subject.
- The news is shocking.
- The police are investigating the case.
- The cattle are grazing.
