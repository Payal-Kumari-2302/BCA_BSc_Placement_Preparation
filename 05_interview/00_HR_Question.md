📚 Interview Questions

🔴 Priority 1 – Must Prepare (15 Questions)

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Why should we hire you?
3. Why do you want to join our company?
4. What are your strengths and weaknesses?
5. Why did you choose BCA?
6. Why did you choose BCA instead of B.Tech?
7. Why do you want to work in the IT industry?
8. What do you know about our company?
9. Explain your academic or final-year project.
10. What are your short-term and long-term career goals?
11. Where do you see yourself in five years?
12. Why should we select you over other candidates?
13. What are your salary expectations?
14. Are you willing to relocate and work night shifts?
15. Do you have any questions for us?

🟠 Priority 2 – Important (12 Questions)

16. What motivates you?
17. How do you handle failure? What did you learn?
18. Tell me about a challenging situation and how you handled it.
19. Do you prefer working alone or in a team?
20. How do you handle criticism?
21. How do you handle stress and work pressure?
22. Are you willing to work in a support or non-technical role?
23. Are you planning to pursue higher studies?
24. What would you do if you received a better job offer?
25. What is the difference between hard work and smart work?
26. What technologies are you currently learning?
27. Do you have any internship experience?

🟡 Priority 3 – Situational Questions (7 Questions)

28. What would you do if your team member was not contributing?
29. What would you do if your manager assigned a task you didn't know?
30. What would you do if your manager gave you a task with a tight deadline?
31. What would you do if you made a mistake in a project?
32. What would you do if your manager rejected your solution?
33. Are you willing to learn new technologies and work outside your skill set?
34. Tell me about a time when you helped someone.

🟢 Priority 4 – TCS Special (4 Questions)

35. What do you know about TCS Ignite?
36. What do you know about TCS Smart Hiring?
37. What is the difference between TCS Ignite and Smart Hiring?
38. What do you know about TCS values and work culture?

🔵 Additional HR Questions (5 Questions)

39. Are you comfortable working from the office?
40. How do you manage your time?
41. How would you describe yourself in three words?
42. What are your hobbies and interests?
43. Is there anything else you would like us to know about you?
