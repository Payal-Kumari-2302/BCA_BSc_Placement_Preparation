# 🔴 PART 1 – ARTICLE

### Definition
An article is a word placed before a noun to show whether it is specific or general.

**Types:** Indefinite Articles – A, An | Definite Article – The | Zero Article – No Article

## Important Article Rules

### Rule 1: Use of A
Use A before a singular countable noun beginning with a consonant sound.  
**Structure:** A + Consonant Sound + Singular Countable Noun  
**Examples:** A book, a teacher, a dog.

### Rule 2: Use of An
Use An before a singular countable noun beginning with a vowel sound.  
**Structure:** An + Vowel Sound + Singular Countable Noun  
**Examples:** An apple, an engineer, an interesting story.

### Rule 3: Articles Depend on Sound
Choose A or An according to pronunciation, not spelling.  
**Structure:** A + Consonant Sound | An + Vowel Sound  
**Examples:** A university, a European, a one-eyed man, an hour, an honest man, an MBA student, an FIR.

### Rule 4: Use of The for Specific Nouns
Use The before a specific noun or a noun already mentioned.  
**Structure:** The + Specific / Previously Mentioned Noun  
**Examples:** I bought a book; the book is interesting. | Please close the door.

### Rule 5: Unique Things
Use The before nouns representing unique things.  
**Structure:** The + Unique Noun  
**Examples:** The Sun, the Moon, the Earth, the Sky.

### Rule 6: Superlative Degree
Use The before superlative adjectives.  
**Structure:** The + Superlative Adjective + Noun  
**Examples:** The best student, the tallest boy, the most beautiful place.

### Rule 7: Ordinal Numbers
Use The before ordinal numbers.  
**Structure:** The + First / Second / Third + Noun  
**Examples:** The first position, the second chapter, the third floor.

### Rule 8: Musical Instruments
Use The before musical instruments when talking about playing them.  
**Structure:** Play + The + Musical Instrument  
**Examples:** Play the guitar, play the piano, play the violin.

### Rule 9: Rivers, Seas and Oceans
Use The before the names of rivers, seas and oceans.  
**Structure:** The + River / Sea / Ocean Name  
**Examples:** The Ganga, the Yamuna, the Indian Ocean, the Arabian Sea, the Nile.

### Rule 10: Mountain Ranges
Use The before the names of mountain ranges.  
**Structure:** The + Mountain Range  
**Examples:** The Himalayas, the Alps, the Andes.

### Rule 11: Proper Nouns
Generally, do not use articles before names of people, cities and countries.  
**Structure:** Proper Noun (No Article)  
**Examples:** Payal lives in India. | Rahul studies in Delhi. | Mumbai is a big city.

### Rule 12: Languages
Do not use articles before language names when referring to them generally.  
**Structure:** Language Name (No Article)  
**Examples:** She speaks English. | He knows Hindi. | I am learning French.

### Rule 13: Meals
Generally, do not use articles before names of meals.  
**Structure:** Have / Eat + Meal Name (No Article)  
**Examples:** Have breakfast, eat lunch, prepare dinner.

### Rule 14: General Plural Nouns
Do not use articles before plural countable nouns when referring to them generally.  
**Structure:** Plural Countable Noun (No Article)  
**Examples:** Students need discipline. | Dogs are faithful animals. | Books provide knowledge.

### Rule 15: Uncountable Nouns
Do not use articles before uncountable nouns when referring to them generally.  
**Structure:** Uncountable Noun (No Article)  
**Examples:** Water is essential. | Honesty is important. | Knowledge is power.

### Rule 16: Professions
Use A or An before professions.  
**Structure:** Subject + Be Verb + A / An + Profession  
**Examples:** She is a doctor. | He is an architect. | My brother is an engineer.

### Rule 17: Exclamatory Sentences
Use A or An before a singular countable noun in exclamations beginning with What.  
**Structure:** What + A / An + Adjective + Singular Noun!  
**Examples:** What a beautiful flower! | What an interesting story!

### Rule 18: The + Adjective
Use The before certain adjectives to represent a group of people.  
**Structure:** The + Adjective = Group of People  
**Examples:** The rich, the poor, the elderly.

### Rule 19: Nationality
Use The before certain nationality words referring to an entire nation or group.  
**Structure:** The + Nationality  
**Examples:** The French, the Japanese, the British.

### Rule 20: Geographical Regions
Use The before geographical regions and directions when used as regions.  
**Structure:** The + East / West / North / South  
**Examples:** The sun rises in the east. | The North is colder than the South. | The Middle East.

### Rule 21: Newspapers
Use The before the names of many newspapers.  
**Structure:** The + Newspaper Name  
**Examples:** The Times of India, The Hindu, The Indian Express.

### Rule 22: Rate and Frequency
Use A or An to express rate, frequency or price.  
**Structure:** A / An + Unit of Measurement  
**Examples:** Twice a day. | ₹100 a kilogram. | 60 kilometres an hour.

### Rule 23: Historical Monuments
Use The before the names of many famous monuments.  
**Structure:** The + Monument Name  
**Examples:** The Taj Mahal, The Red Fort, The Qutub Minar.

### Rule 24: Singular Countable Nouns
A singular countable noun generally needs an article or another determiner.  
**Structure:** A / An / The / My / This + Singular Countable Noun  
**Examples:** A student, my book, this umbrella.

### Rule 25: The + Same
Use The before Same.  
**Structure:** The + Same + Noun  
**Examples:** The same class, the same problem, the same dress.

### Rule 26: The + Whole
Use The before Whole when referring to an entire thing or group.  
**Structure:** The + Whole + Noun  
**Examples:** The whole book, the whole day, the whole country.

### Rule 27: A + Hundred / Thousand / Million
Use A before hundred, thousand and million in number expressions.  
**Structure:** A + Hundred / Thousand / Million + Noun  
**Examples:** A hundred students, a thousand people, a million dollars.

### Rule 28: The + Only
Use The before Only when it identifies a particular person or thing.  
**Structure:** The + Only + Noun  
**Examples:** The only son, the only solution, the only candidate.

### Rule 29: The + First / Last
Use The before First and Last when they identify a particular position or order.  
**Structure:** The + First / Last + Noun  
**Examples:** The first person, the last candidate, the first chapter.

### Rule 30: Zero Article with Institutions
Use no article before certain institutions when referring to their primary purpose or activity.  
**Structure:** Go To / Be At + Institution (No Article)  
**Examples:** Go to school. | Go to college. | Be in prison.  
**Note:** Use The when referring to a specific building or a different purpose: I went to the school to meet the principal.

# 🔴 PART 2 – SPECIAL ARTICLE RULES

### Rule 1: A / An + Adjective + Noun
Choose A or An according to the sound of the adjective immediately following it.  
**Structure:** A / An + Adjective + Noun  
**Examples:** A beautiful girl, an intelligent student, a useful book, an old man.

### Rule 2: The + Comparative Degree
Use The in comparative structures showing that two changes are connected.  
**Structure:** The + Comparative, The + Comparative  
**Examples:** The more you practise, the better you become. | The harder you work, the more successful you become.

### Rule 3: A Few / A Little
Use A Few with plural countable nouns and A Little with uncountable nouns.  
**Structure:** A Few + Plural Noun | A Little + Uncountable Noun  
**Examples:** A few students attended. | I have a little water.

### Rule 4: Few / Little
Use Few with plural countable nouns and Little with uncountable nouns to indicate an insufficient quantity.  
**Structure:** Few + Plural Noun | Little + Uncountable Noun  
**Examples:** Few people attended. | Little time is left.

### Rule 5: Such + A / An
Use A or An after Such before a singular countable noun.  
**Structure:** Such + A / An + Adjective + Singular Noun  
**Examples:** Such a beautiful place! | Such an intelligent boy!

### Rule 6: Quite + A / An
Use A or An after Quite before a singular countable noun.  
**Structure:** Quite + A / An + Adjective + Singular Noun  
**Examples:** Quite a difficult question. | Quite an interesting story.

### Rule 7: Rather + A / An
Use A or An after Rather before a singular countable noun.  
**Structure:** Rather + A / An + Adjective + Singular Noun  
**Examples:** Rather a difficult task. | Rather an unusual situation.

### Rule 8: Mountain Peaks
Generally, do not use The before individual mountain peaks.  
**Structure:** Mount + Individual Peak Name (No Article)  
**Examples:** Mount Everest, Mount Kilimanjaro.

### Rule 9: Groups of Islands
Use The before the names of groups of islands.  
**Structure:** The + Group of Islands  
**Examples:** The Andaman and Nicobar Islands, the Maldives, the Philippines.

### Rule 10: Deserts
Use The before the names of deserts.  
**Structure:** The + Desert Name  
**Examples:** The Sahara Desert, the Thar Desert, the Gobi Desert.

### Rule 11: Certain Country Names
Use The before country names containing words such as Kingdom, States or Republic, and certain plural country names.  
**Structure:** The + Qualifying Country Name  
**Examples:** The United States, the United Kingdom, the United Arab Emirates, the Netherlands.

# 🔴 PART 5 – MIXED ARTICLE ERROR SPOTTING PRACTICE

**Target Exams:** TCS Ignite | TCS NQT | Wipro | Infosys | Accenture | Cognizant | Deloitte | Genpact  
**Instructions:** Identify the grammatically incorrect part of each sentence.  
**Options:** A) First Part | B) Second Part | C) Third Part | D) No Error

## LEVEL 1 – BASIC

**Q1.** (A) He is / (B) an university student / (C) of this college / (D) No error.  
**Answer: B** – He is a university student of this college.

**Q2.** (A) She is / (B) a honest girl / (C) in our class / (D) No error.  
**Answer: B** – She is an honest girl in our class.

**Q3.** (A) I bought / (B) an umbrella / (C) yesterday / (D) No error.  
**Answer: D**

**Q4.** (A) He is / (B) best player / (C) in the team / (D) No error.  
**Answer: B** – He is the best player in the team.

**Q5.** (A) She speaks / (B) the English / (C) fluently / (D) No error.  
**Answer: B** – She speaks English fluently.

**Q6.** (A) The Ganga / (B) is a holy / (C) river in India / (D) No error.  
**Answer: D**

**Q7.** (A) He plays / (B) guitar / (C) every evening / (D) No error.  
**Answer: B** – He plays the guitar every evening.

**Q8.** (A) I had / (B) a breakfast / (C) at 8 AM / (D) No error.  
**Answer: B** – I had breakfast at 8 AM.

**Q9.** (A) India is / (B) an beautiful / (C) country / (D) No error.  
**Answer: B** – India is a beautiful country.

**Q10.** (A) He is / (B) an engineer / (C) in this company / (D) No error.  
**Answer: D**
